How it works

Six steps from one site video to a buying decision. Open any step for the details.

01CapturePhone video, RGB-D or LiDAR of the site.

One walkthrough is enough. The scan in this page came from a robot-mounted RGB-D camera, 353 frames.

INPUT · video or photos · depth and LiDAR optional

02ReconstructCamera poses and a dense 3D map.

Heavy reconstruction runs on a cloud GPU. Output is a mesh in real units, floor and walls included.

OUTPUT · poses · point cloud · mesh

03Build the sceneA MuJoCo scene with simple collision shapes.

The floor is leveled, walls become boxes and clutter becomes convex pieces, so a robot can walk and bump into them.

EXPORT · MuJoCo · Isaac Sim · URDF

04CheckCan we trust the geometry?

We score the reconstruction against laser scans and walk a G1 into every wall to make sure nothing passes through.

SCORED ON · ETH3D · Hilti 2022

05Run the taskThe robot does the job in the copy of your site.

Today a G1 walks in reconstructed scenes. Next: two G1s carrying a box, G1 manipulation and a VLA-driven arm.

ROBOTS · Unitree G1 · Go1 · Panda arm

A short report: does it fit, how fast is it, what does it cost, and when does it pay back.

Robots from different owners, working together.

A neighbor's robot can ask yours for help. Yours checks who is asking and decides how far to follow.

  1. VERIFYIs it really that robot? Signed messages, no replays, no strangers.
  2. DECIDEHow far do we follow it? Allow, partial or deny, per action.
  3. ACTOnly allowed actions reach the robot. Some are never allowed.
  4. LOGThe owner sees one plain line per request.
